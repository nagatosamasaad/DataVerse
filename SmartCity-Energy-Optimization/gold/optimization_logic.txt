# Optimization Engine Logic  
Smart City Energy Optimization – Gold Layer

This document explains the logic used to generate optimization recommendations
based on forecasted energy consumption, weather impact, and traffic congestion.


---

## 1. Objective
The Optimization Engine is responsible for:

- Identifying peak load hours  
- Detecting high-risk regions  
- Suggesting actions to reduce energy stress  
- Improving city-wide energy stability  
- Prioritizing critical areas during high-demand periods  

The engine operates entirely on **Gold layer tables** generated from ML forecasts.

---

## 2. Inputs
The optimization engine reads from the following Gold tables:

### ✔ `gold.energy_forecast`
- timestamp  
- region_id  
- predicted_consumption  
- historical_peak  
- prediction_error  

### ✔ `gold.risk_zones`
- region_id  
- timestamp  
- risk_level (Low / Medium / High)

### ✔ Weather / Traffic Impact (Silver master table)
- temperature  
- humidity  
- traffic_congestion  

---

## 3. Core Logic

### 3.1 Detecting Predicted Peaks
IF predicted_consumption >= 0.9 * historical_peak:
mark as HIGH RISK
ELSE IF predicted_consumption >= 0.75 * historical_peak:
mark as MEDIUM RISK
ELSE:
mark as LOW RISK


### 3.2 Peak Shifting Recommendations
- Delay charging EV fleets to off-peak hours  
- Reduce HVAC usage between 12pm–6pm  
- Activate solar storage systems  
- Move non-critical operations (e.g., irrigation pumps) to nighttime  

### 3.3 Critical Region Priority
Regions categorized as "critical":

- Hospitals  
- Fire stations  
- Data centers  
- Transportation hubs  

Logic:
IF region is critical AND risk_level = HIGH:
increase energy allocation
minimize load shifting for this region

### 3.4 Load Balancing Between Neighboring Zones
The engine compares region clusters:
IF Region A (High Risk) AND Region B (Low Risk):
shift partial load from A → B


Example:
- Move cooling load from Zone 1 to Zone 2  
- Balance street lighting timing between regions  

### 3.5 Weather-Aware Optimization
IF temperature > 35°C:
recommend reducing HVAC usage
IF humidity > 80%:
increase ventilation before peak hours


### 3.6 Traffic-Aware Optimization
IF traffic_congestion >= 7:
limit EV charging in congested zones

---

## 4. Output Table: gold.optimization_recommendations

The final table contains:

- timestamp  
- region_id  
- predicted_peak_time  
- risk_level  
- recommendation_text  
- priority_score  

Example:

| region_id | timestamp | risk | recommendation |
|-----------|-----------|-------|----------------|
| R1        | 2025-11-27 18:00 | HIGH | Reduce HVAC load and shift EV charging to midnight.|
